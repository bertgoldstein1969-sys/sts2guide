FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about STS2.

When does Slay the Spire 2 come out?

Slay the Spire 2 launches in Steam Early Access on March 5, 2026. It will be available on PC via Steam first. A full release window has not been announced.

Does Slay the Spire 2 have multiplayer?

Yes! STS2 introduces 4-player co-op — a first for the series. You and up to 3 friends can tackle the Spire together, coordinating decks and relics.

Is Slay the Spire 2 on console or mobile?

The March 5 Early Access launch is PC only (Steam). Console and mobile versions have not been announced for Early Access, but Mega Crit released STS1 on all platforms eventually.

Do I need to play STS1 first?

No — STS2 is designed as a standalone entry. That said, STS1 veterans will recognize many mechanics and returning characters. We recommend it, but it's not required.

How much content is in Early Access at launch?

Mega Crit confirmed the launch build includes new cards, characters, events, relics, potions, abilities, alternate acts, and more. It's a substantial launch — typical of MegaCrit's quality bar.

Will this site be updated after Early Access launches?

Yes. We'll update guides, tier lists, and the wiki as Mega Crit adds content and balance patches throughout Early Access.
