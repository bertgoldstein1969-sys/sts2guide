What's Included at Early Access Launch

Mega Crit confirmed the launch build is substantial. Here's what's coming on March 5:

  • ✅ New and returning characters (Slayers with distinct card pools)
  • ✅ New cards — expanded card pools across all characters
  • ✅ New relics — new passive items to discover
  • ✅ New events — story events across the Spire
  • ✅ New potions — expanded potion selection
  • ✅ New abilities — new character mechanics
  • ✅ Alternate Acts — new branching act paths beyond the original 3
  • ✅ Ancients — new meta-progression mechanic
  • ✅ 4-Player Co-op — full multiplayer at launch
  • ✅ Procedurally generated maps — branching paths, elites, bosses
  • 🔜 More content added throughout Early Access (MegaCrit's model)

The "Secret Thursday in March" Story

In September 2025, MegaCrit promised STS2 would launch on "a secret Thursday in March" — one of gaming's more memorable release window announcements. The secret Thursday turned out to be March 5, revealed just two weeks before launch.

This was very on-brand for Mega Crit: understated, confident, and with a wink at their community.

Why Early Access?

Mega Crit explained their reasoning directly on the Steam page:

"Slay the Spire's success comes from our community!" — MegaCrit on why they're using Early Access. STS1 launched in Early Access in November 2017 and released fully in January 2019. That model produced one of the best-reviewed games of the decade.

Expect STS2 to follow a similar path: launch with strong content, iterate based on community feedback, and add characters/mechanics over 12-24 months before full release.

What to Expect at Launch

Based on MegaCrit's track record with STS1 Early Access:

  • Solid playable experience — their Early Access launches are polished
  • Regular balance patches — expect hotfixes in the first week
  • Active community feedback integration — they listen to Reddit and Discord
  • Content additions over months — new characters, cards, acts
  • Price increase at full release — buy early if you're interested

Should You Buy Early Access?

Short answer: yes, if you liked STS1. MegaCrit is one of the most trusted indie developers for Early Access. STS1's EA period was widely considered one of the best in gaming history — they communicated well, updated frequently, and delivered on their promises.
